Frequently asked questions

How do I delete my account and data?

You can delete your account at any time from within the Nine Men's Morris app, under Profile → Settings. This permanently removes your profile and associated personal data. You can also email us at tomatillo.llc@gmail.com and we will process your deletion request. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are billed through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Manage or cancel them in your device's App Store / Google Play account settings under Subscriptions. Refund requests are handled by Apple or Google per their policies.

I found a bug or something isn't working. What should I do?

Please email us with your device model, operating system version, and a short description of what happened. Screenshots help a lot. You can also use the in-app "report a problem" option during a game.

How do I report another player or inappropriate content?

Use the report option within the app, or email us with the player's username and details. We review reports and take action against conduct that violates our Terms & Conditions.

What are Golden Pips?

Golden Pips are the in-app currency, used to unlock cosmetic items. They have no real-world monetary value and can't be exchanged for cash. See our Terms & Conditions for full details.
